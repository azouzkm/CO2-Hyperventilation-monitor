Phone alerts

Get the monitor's alarms as a message on your phone, through CallMeBot. No patient information is ever sent, your number stays on this laptop, and you can stop the messages here at any time.

When a message comes

  • CriticalAt once. Again after 2 minutes if nobody has acknowledged it, and then the backups get it too.
  • AdvisoryIf it lasts a minute.
  • TechnicalIf it lasts a minute: a sensor or link problem means the patient is not being watched.
  • EndedOne short message when an alarm you were told about is over.
